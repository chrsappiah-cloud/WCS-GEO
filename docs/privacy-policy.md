# WCS Mining AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

WCS Mining AI is provided by World Class Scholars to support mineral exploration planning, ranked target review, map-based project context, and field observation workflows.

## Information We Collect

WCS Mining AI may collect or process the following information when you use the app:

- Account and organization access information used to sign in and select an organization.
- Project and exploration workflow data such as selected projects, ranked targets, field notes, and sync state.
- Approximate or precise device location when you grant location permission, used for field observations and map context.
- Technical diagnostics needed to operate, secure, debug, and improve the app.

The app does not collect health, medical, financial, or biometric information.

## How We Use Information

Information is used to:

- Provide secure access to organization-specific exploration workflows.
- Display project dashboards, target rankings, maps, evidence provenance, and field note workflows.
- Save local field observations and sync them when online.
- Maintain app reliability, security, and lifecycle behavior.

## Location Data

If you allow location access, WCS Mining AI uses location to support field observations and map context. Location access is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings at any time.

## Storage and Retention

Demo data may be stored locally on the device during app use. Organization or project data may be stored using configured backend services such as Supabase where enabled by the organization. Data is retained only as needed to provide the app workflow, support auditability, comply with applicable obligations, or until deleted by the organization or user where deletion is available.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is not shared with third parties for advertising. Service providers may process information only as needed to provide hosting, authentication, sync, diagnostics, or security services.

## Security

WCS Mining AI uses platform security features and backend access controls to protect app and organization data. Users should keep their device passcode, Apple ID, and organization credentials secure.

## Children

WCS Mining AI is intended for professional, business, education, and exploration workflow use. It is not directed to children.
